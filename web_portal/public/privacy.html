Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 2026

NERMAI IAS Academy ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, use, and safeguard personal information when you access or use the NERMAI IAS Academy mobile applications (Android/iOS) and web portal.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or interact with our platform, we collect:

  • Account Information: Full name, phone number, email address, and Google profile details (when signing in via Google).
  • Academic Data: Enrolled courses, batch assignments, attendance records, mock test attempts, scores, and progress analytics.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device push tokens (for sending academic notifications) and general device identifiers required for secure session management.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to deliver and improve our educational services, including:

  • Providing access to course study materials, question banks, and live sessions.
  • Conducting online mock exams and generating individual performance reports.
  • Sending important notices about exam dates, class schedules, and academic updates.
  • Assisting with student inquiries and technical support.

3. Camera & Microphone Permissions

Sensitive Permission Notice:
Our mobile app may request access to your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) and Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO). These permissions are strictly and exclusively used for student participation in interactive live online classes. We do not record, collect, or store personal audio or video outside active live class sessions.

4. Push Notifications

With your explicit permission, we use push notifications to send reminders for test submissions, scheduled webinars, daily quizzes, and administrative announcements. You may opt out of notifications at any time via your device's application settings.

5. Data Security & Storage

All user information is transmitted using TLS/SSL encryption and securely hosted on Google Firebase infrastructure. Robust access controls and security rules guarantee that your personal data is accessible only to you and authorized academy administrative personnel.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party marketers or advertisers under any circumstances. Data is only shared with trusted service providers (such as Google Firebase) strictly for hosting and notifications.

7. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your personal data or account. To request data deletion or account removal, please email us directly at the contact address below.
